# Resale IQ

**Reseller toolkit: what to pay, what to ask, what you keep.**

Resale IQ is a toolkit for people who buy second-hand to resell on Vinted, eBay, Depop, Poshmark, Mercari, Etsy and other marketplaces. Before you buy: type a brand and model to get BUY, WATCH or SKIP, the typical asking price and the most to pay, from public Vinted listings in Spain, France, Germany, Italy and Portugal (asking prices, not sale prices). Before you list: fee calculators for each platform, a shipping comparer, lot and bundle calculators, a photo tool and the Listing Studio, which writes titles and descriptions in six languages. When a buyer makes an offer, the offer planner gives your walk-away floor and a reply. After the sale, the profit ledger shows what you kept, with tax-year files and the EU DAC7 lines. Free: 10 checks a day with no account or card; the calculators are free. Seller €19 a month, Pro €49 a month, 7-day Pro trial with no card.
